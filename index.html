The concept

Price shows movement.
Volume adds context.

A chart can move without offering a clear setup. TCJ Smart Volume Concept uses the relationship between price and traded volume to identify significant activity and establish structural reference areas.

The method centres on Structural Control Candles—defined areas where you can examine whether price respects, tests or breaks the structure.

Developed by TheCoinJob, SVC brings coin discovery and chart analysis into one practical workflow: find a market worth examining, then decide whether its structure offers a trade.

Quick glossary

What is a Control Candle?

A candle selected because its price-and-volume strength stands out against recent activity. Its boundaries become a reference area for watching what price does next.

Finding the candle is the first step. Later confirmed closes beyond its body boundaries establish Bullish or Bearish Control. Control can also be absent or become invalid.

A Control label gives you context. It is not, by itself, an instruction to enter a trade.

Before you begin

A few things to know.

Is Zero Scanner a trading signal service?

It is a crypto data analysis and discovery tool. It filters and ranks market readings so you can find coins to examine. It does not place trades or promise that a ranked coin will move in your favour.

Which markets does it cover?

The scanner currently uses Bybit and Binance perpetual futures data. It is focused on crypto, not forex or other markets. If you trade elsewhere, check that exchange’s chart, liquidity and contract details before acting.

Do I need to pay or create an account?

The scanner is currently free to use and does not require an account. You can open it directly from this page.

Why use Structural Control Candles?

They give this method consistent reference areas drawn from price-and-volume activity, with defined rules for establishing and invalidating Control. They help organise chart analysis without assuming that any level must hold.
