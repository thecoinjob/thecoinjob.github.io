What is a Control Candle?

Control Candles, or CCs, are significant candles that appear when unusually strong market pressure steps in. They are not defined by price size alone. Strong volume and a high Force Index help identify aggressive buying or selling activity.

Think of a CC as a footprint left by a strong push in the market. Its price area becomes a reference point. From there, you watch whether price respects the area or violates it.

How is it found?

The detection uses price and traded volume data supplied by the exchange. Force Index brings those readings together to measure the strength of the activity. The system compares that strength with recent candles to find activity that stands out.

These defined rules make selection consistent instead of relying on appearance alone. They provide evidence of pressure; they do not reveal individual traders’ identities or intentions.

What does Control mean?

Bullish Control

Buyers have control while price holds above the bullish Control Candle. A confirmed close below the candle body invalidates that bullish control.

Bearish Control

Sellers have control while price holds below the bearish Control Candle. A confirmed close above the candle body invalidates that bearish control.

No Control means the system has no valid established bullish or bearish control to report. Finding an important candle and establishing directional control are separate steps.

A Control label gives you context. Examine the chart and identify your trade’s invalidation before entering.

Familiar with order blocks?

You already understand the idea of watching a price area for a reaction. Control Candles serve a similar practical purpose. In SVC, that reference is selected through price-and-volume strength, using exchange data and defined rules.

TheCoinJob uses CCs to give chart analysis a consistent foundation. The question is not whether a level must hold. It is how price behaves when it reaches that area.

How I find and assess a coin

  1. Start with Zero Scanner. Use price, volume, open interest and your chosen filters to find candidates showing activity. Control, Participation and Crowd Read add context.
  2. Open the chart. Examine price around the Control Candle. Is price respecting the area, testing it or breaking through it?
  3. Decide whether the setup makes sense. Define your entry, invalidation and target. If the chart is messy or offers no clear setup, move on.

A high scanner ranking is a reason to investigate. It is not a reason to enter automatically.
